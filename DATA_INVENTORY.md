## 6. What you must provide to regenerate the REAL numbers

1. UK Biobank approved application + RAP access (App 1002450), **or** the local `ukb_FULL_MASTER.csv`.
2. All-Wales FH Registry data-sharing approval + `pass_FULL_MASTER.csv` and Dragon-3 extract.
3. Run **locally** (never on a third-party cloud): `python TUDOR_MASTER.py --real --all`.

Without (1)+(2), the most you can reproduce is the **method** on synthetic data — which is
exactly what Claude Code on the web is for (see `REPRODUCE_ON_CLAUDE_WEB.md`).
